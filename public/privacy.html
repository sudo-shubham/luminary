Privacy Policy — Luminary

Last updated: November 27, 2026 • Applies to Web PWA & Android App (io.github.sudoshubham.luminary)
← Back to Luminary

1. Summary

Luminary is a 100% client-side, open-source photography assistant. We do not operate user accounts, backend databases, analytics trackers, or advertising SDKs. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your preferences and photography logs are stored locally on your own device using browser localStorage:

  • Selected theme (Dark / Daylight), camera brand/sensor profile, lens selection, and shooting mode.
  • Custom camera bodies, custom lenses, and Shot Journal entries (including any notes you write).

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export a JSON backup file yourself.

3. Optional Location & Device Sensors

  • GPS Location: Only accessed when you tap the GPS detect button. Your coordinates are used locally for solar/lunar calculations and sent directly from your browser to public weather/map services (listed below) to fetch forecasts and nearby viewpoints.
  • Compass / Orientation Sensors: Only accessed when you tap Sync Compass to align the on-screen light radar. Sensor readings are processed strictly in memory on your device and never transmitted.

4. Third-Party Public Services

When online, Luminary makes direct client-side requests to free public APIs to provide weather, maps, and scenic spot discovery:

  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com, geocoding-api.open-meteo.com) — weather forecasts and city search.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim / Overpass API (overpass-api.de, nominatim.openstreetmap.org, tile.openstreetmap.org) — reverse geocoding, map tiles, and nearby scenic spots.
  • BigDataCloud (api.bigdatacloud.net) — reverse geocoding fallback.
  • Wikipedia & Wikimedia Commons (en.wikipedia.org, commons.wikimedia.org, upload.wikimedia.org) — scenic spot fallback and spot thumbnails.

These providers receive standard HTTP request metadata (such as IP address and requested coordinates/search query) subject to their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Luminary does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Contact & Source Code

Luminary is open-source. You can inspect the source code or ask privacy questions via GitHub Issues: github.com/sudo-shubham/luminary/issues.
